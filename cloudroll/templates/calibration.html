Cloud-cover calibration

Each night CloudRoll keeps every dark minute (sun below {{ sun_below }}°): the IR sensor's raw frame and settings next to the allsky camera's star count, plus a few allsky images for review. A clear moonless sky shows ~{{ clear_stars }}+ stars; overcast shows almost none. Use this page to check whether the sensor's Clear Δ / Overcast Δ still fit, and to try others before changing them on the sensor.

Replays the sensor's per-pixel cloud cover over the selected minutes with other thresholds (to within ~0.5 %). A non-zero ambient slope shifts both thresholds with temperature – the correction proposed for the firmware.

X axis:

Each dot is one dark minute: the median Δ over the sensor's cloud region, coloured by the allsky star count. Horizontal lines are the sensor's thresholds (solid) and the what-if pair (dashed). The sloped line is a straight-line fit through the clear minutes: if clear skies drift away from the solid Clear Δ line as the season changes, it's time to re-tune.

Image review

Each night keeps about a dozen allsky images: some where the sensor and the star count disagree most, one from each star-count band, and some at random. Label what you see; labels are saved straight away and become ground truth that's better than star count for thin cloud, smoke or dew.
